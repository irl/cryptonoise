## Getting Started

This is a list of resources you can use for better
privacy when communicating online or surfing the web.

### Tor Browser

A browser that prevents whoever is watching your
Internet connection from learning what sites you visit, prevents the sites
you visit from learning your physical location, and lets you access sites which
might otherwise be censored. You can download it from the official
[Tor Browser page](https://www.torproject.org/projects/torbrowser.html.en#downloads).

### Tails

Tails is a live
    operating system that allows you to circumvent censorship, browse anonymously
    whilst leaving no trace on the computer you are using it on. It can start on
    almost any computer from a CD, DVD or USB. Follow its installation guide on the
    [Tails website](https://tails.boum.org/install/os/index.en.html).

### privacytools.io

[privacytools.io](https://privacytools.io/) provide services,
    tools, and knowledge to protect your privacy against global mass surveillance,
    and moderate a thriving community of privacy-minded individuals like yourself
    to discuss and learn about new advances in protecting your online data.
